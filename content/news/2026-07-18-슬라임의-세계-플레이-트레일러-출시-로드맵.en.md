Team42 has released a new gameplay trailer for its MMORPG World of Slime along with a global release roadmap. Under the plan, a demo version opens on Steam this August, mobile pre-orders follow in November, and full service begins simultaneously on PC and mobile in December.

The video emphasizes the game's MMORPG direction, showing multiple players riding slimes, enjoying life content such as fishing, and taking on large bosses in cooperative combat. The developers note that where the May showing at Play Expo focused on capturing, fusing, and growing slimes, this trailer broadens the appeal toward cooperative play between users.

The distinctive art style has spread through Reddit and social media, sustaining interest in overseas markets including the United States, Brazil, and Indonesia. Team42 plans to keep sharing development updates through channels such as its official Discord while polishing the game ahead of launch.
