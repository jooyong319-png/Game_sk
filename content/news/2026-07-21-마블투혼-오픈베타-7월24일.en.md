[Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls](/game/marvel-tokon-2026), a new fighting title in development at Arc System Works, kicks off its open beta test on July 24. The test runs from 4 PM KST on the 24th through the 26th and lets players try 15 characters, including newcomer Blade, across six stages. It also offers online casual and ranked matches, local versus, a training mode, and three story-mode chapters.

Marvel Tokon is built around a 4-on-4 tag system in which four characters share a single health bar under a best-of-five, first-to-three format. Players can spend the assemble gauge to summon an assist character via "Crossover," shifting the flow of a match. The full launch is set for August 6.
