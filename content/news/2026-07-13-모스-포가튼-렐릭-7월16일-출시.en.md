Polyarc's [Moss: The Forgotten Relic](/game/moss-the-forgotten-relic-20260716) launches on July 16. Supported platforms are Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One, and Steam. The game weaves together the stories of Moss and Moss: Book II, both originally VR exclusives, and it is not a straight port: the camera work and staging have been rebuilt so the games can be played without a VR headset.

Adding the Twilight Garden expansion content to the two original chapters, the release wraps up the journey of the mouse knight Quill in a single package, with new cutscenes and platform-specific graphical improvements. A demo went up on June 8 to coincide with Steam Next Fest.
