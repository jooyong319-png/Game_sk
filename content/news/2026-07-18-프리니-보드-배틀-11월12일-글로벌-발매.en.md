Nippon Ichi Software's new party game [Let's Play Together! Prinny Board Battle](/game/prinny-board-battle-20261112) launches on November 12. The Korean, Japanese, North American, and European versions all release simultaneously worldwide, with support for Nintendo Switch 2, Nintendo Switch as a download-only title, and PlayStation 5. The announcement came alongside the official site and a first promotional video.

The game stars Prinny, Nippon Ichi's signature character, and combines command RPG battles with board game progression. Even when several players join forces to bring down a boss, only the one who drags that boss back to the starting point wins, so cooperation and betrayal keep trading places right up to the final moment. The Korean and traditional Chinese versions are priced at 66,700 won for both physical and download editions, rated for ages 15 and up.
