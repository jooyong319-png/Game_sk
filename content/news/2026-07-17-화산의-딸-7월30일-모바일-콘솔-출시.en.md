The raising simulation Volcano Princess expands to mobile and console on July 30, arriving on iOS, Android, PS5, and Nintendo Switch, with CyberAgent subsidiary Colorful Palette publishing. The original came from Chinese indie team Egg Hatcher, released on Steam in 2023, and tells the story of a protagonist who lost his wife raising his daughter in the Volcano Kingdom amid deepening conflict between humans and demons. How you raise her determines her future, with more than 50 endings.

This multiplatform version goes beyond a simple port. The Japanese and English translations have been reworked and traditional Chinese added, while the Japanese version is fully voiced, adding Marina Inoue and Kento Ito alongside Mai Nakahara, who has voiced the daughter since 2023. New story content, still images, and a new dungeon open as well. For existing Steam players, additional content will be delivered through free updates in stages starting this October.
