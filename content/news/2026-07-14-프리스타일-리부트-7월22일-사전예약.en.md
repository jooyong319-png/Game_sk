Joycity announced that pre-registration for [FreeStyle Reboot](/game/freestyle-reboot-2026), the direct successor to its flagship FreeStyle IP, opens in Korea on July 22 ahead of the domestic launch. The game preserves the freewheeling street basketball feel and real-time 3-on-3 competition of the original while raising visual quality on a new engine and improving player convenience. The company says it prioritized building a fair competitive environment decided purely by skill.

Having already launched in Taiwan to validate the gameplay, Joycity plans to use the Korean release as a springboard for a staged rollout across China and other global markets. Pre-registration is available through the official website and app stores.
