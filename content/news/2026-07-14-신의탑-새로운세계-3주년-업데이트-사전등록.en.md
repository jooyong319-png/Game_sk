Netmarble announced that its mobile RPG [Tower of God: New World](/game/tower-of-god-3rd-anniv-20260722) will receive a major third-anniversary update on July 22. The company opened pre-registration in line with the update schedule, and participants receive 1,000 Nonstop SSR+ tickets plus growth material selection boxes on the day of the update. On top of that, an additional 2,000 Nonstop SSR+ tickets are planned as a third-anniversary event reward.

For the summer season, vacation outfits for Sia Sia, Hwaryun, and Yuri Jahad return to the shop, and story events and other in-game content are being prepared alongside. Netmarble appears to be using the anniversary as a hook to draw players in during the summer.
