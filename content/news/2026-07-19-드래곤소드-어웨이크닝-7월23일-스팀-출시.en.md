HOUND13's open-world action RPG [Dragonsword: Awakening](/game/dragonsword-awakening-20260723) launches on Steam at 7 AM KST on July 23. The title reworks the studio's former live-service game 'Dragonsword' into a gacha-free, buy-to-play single-player package. After ending its publishing deal with Webzen and shifting to self-publishing, HOUND13 released a demo in June and has now unveiled a launch trailer ahead of release.

The new trailer showcases skills and combos from newly added playable heroes such as Othello, Theresia, and Dana, along with the game's signature **tag action** system of swapping between characters and climbing onto giant bosses to attack. The developer says it refined the game after the demo, adding a lock-on system, sensitivity and aim-assist options, and improved jump and controller handling based on player feedback. The game passed 300,000 Steam wishlists on July 10.
