Nexon has applied the first large-scale update of its summer campaign RE:ACTION to the MMORPG [Mabinogi](/game/mabinogi-reaction-1st-arcana-awakening-20260709). The centerpiece is **Arcana Awakening**, which pushes character growth past its previous ceiling, along with an **Ogham Word** system built around 26 ancient letters. Arcana Awakening unlocks after completing a guide quest, and this first update allows growth up to level 30, with the cap rising to 50 in a planned second update.

Quality-of-life saw a heavy pass as well. The Near system used for dungeon entry has been converted to a pass purchase model, and inheritance costs between identical equipment are now free. A Fever Season event for newcomers and returning players also began, offering nine weeks of benefits including weapon and armor grants and a free daily rebirth.
