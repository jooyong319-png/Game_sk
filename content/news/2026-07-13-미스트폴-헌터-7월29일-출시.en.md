[Mistfall Hunter](/game/mistfall-hunter-20260729), a third-person extraction action RPG developed by Veiling Games and published by Skystone Games, launches July 29 on PC via Steam, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S. The team locked in the previously unannounced date with a new trailer at the Future Games Show Summer Showcase. Set in a world where the gods have fallen and golden mist blankets the land, the game casts players as an exiled warrior known as a Guildhunter, repeating cycles of high-risk exploration and life-or-death extraction.

Class-specific skills and demanding boss fights are the emphasis, and the game drew attention earlier with strong concurrent player numbers during its Steam Next Fest demo. Because of the time difference, Korean players can expect Steam access to open in the early hours of July 30 local time.
