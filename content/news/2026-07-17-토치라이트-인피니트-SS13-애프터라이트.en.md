Torchlight: Infinite, the cross-platform hack-and-slash operated by XD Entertainment, begins its thirteenth season, SS13 Afterlight, on July 17. The season revolves around Funeral Procession Escort play, where players activate coffins scattered across another realm and light memorial lanterns to destroy evil spirits. Clearing spirits faster summons stronger enemies and scales up rewards, and variables such as golden spirits, special coffins, extra waves, and hidden bosses keep each run different.

The new hero trait, Celena of Dancing with the Sea, can use ground skills while moving and unleashes area attacks with crimson shadows. Alongside it come progression and challenge content including engraving talent enhancement through the Netherlord's divinity system and Ascension Trials, which require clearing maps consecutively within a time limit.

Lunaria, previously seasonal content, has moved to permanent content to reduce the leveling burden, and convenience features such as market price lookups, mapping material presets, and batch settlement of trade earnings are included. A new main city and graphical renewal are applied, and XD is running a login event granting a legendary battle spirit selection package and a ten-pull summon ticket to mark the update.
