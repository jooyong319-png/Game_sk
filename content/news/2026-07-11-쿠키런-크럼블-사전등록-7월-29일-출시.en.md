Devsisters has opened global pre-registration for its new idle RPG [Cookie Run: Crumble](/game/cookierun-crumble-20260729) as it prepares for a July 29 launch. Built by Studio Kingdom, the game pairs an idle system where cookies grow on their own with the strategic layer of combining cookies and pets. Pre-registration is available on Google Play and the Apple App Store as well as Galaxy Store and ONE Store.

Devsisters says it will hand out tiered rewards as cumulative sign-ups pass 100,000, 300,000, 500,000, and 1 million, including crystals, coins, premium cookie cutters, and premium pet summon tickets. Rewards will be delivered through the in-game mailbox after launch.
