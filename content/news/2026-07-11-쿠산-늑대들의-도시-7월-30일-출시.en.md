[KUSAN: City of Wolves](/game/kusan-city-of-wolves-20260730), a hardcore top-down action game the Korean studio CIRCLEfromDOT spent roughly eight years building, launches on July 30. It arrives simultaneously on PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch in digital form, with physical editions also planned for PS5 and Switch. PQube handles global distribution.

The game is a high-speed top-down shooter clearly influenced by Hotline Miami. Players take the role of Jin, a former soldier turned mercenary in the rain-soaked city of Kusan, tasked with protecting a girl carrying a nuclear device capable of leveling the entire city. One-hit-kill combat and stylish chained takedowns reminiscent of John Wick define the experience.
