Nexon will launch [Higan: Eruthyll](/game/higan-eruthyll-kr-2026), a collectible subculture RPG developed by China's Bilibili, in Korea on July 23. Set in a dark fantasy world where magic and science coexist, players lead a troupe on a quest to reclaim a lost paradise. The game blends real-time 3D combat with card-based tactics, and leans on cinematic anime-style cutscenes and full Japanese voice acting, while an idle system keeps the day-to-day load light. The Korean build is the 2.0 remake, which heavily overhauls the gameplay and character balance, and runs on both mobile and PC.

Higan: Eruthyll is the debut title under 'select all', a new publishing brand from Nexon. After its April 2023 global release, the 2.0 version rolled out first in China and then in Taiwan, Hong Kong and Macau, and this Korean launch widens its service footprint further. Pre-registration is underway ahead of release.
