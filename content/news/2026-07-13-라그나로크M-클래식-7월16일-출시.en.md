Gravity has confirmed July 16 as the Korean launch date for its new MMORPG [Ragnarok M: Classic](/game/ragnarok-m-classic-20260716). The game services PC and mobile simultaneously. It went through a closed beta test in Korea in June, and on July 12, four days before launch, Gravity held a user meetup called Fun and Fan Day in Gangnam, Seoul, inviting CBT participants and influencers for direct conversation with the development team.

Ragnarok M: Classic follows Ragnarok M: Eternal Love, carrying over the classic feel of the original while aiming for a lighter play experience. Gravity says the in-game economy is built around Zeny as the core currency so that players can focus on progression and adventure. Domestic pre-registration is running until launch.
