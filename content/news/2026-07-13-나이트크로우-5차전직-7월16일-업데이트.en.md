Wemade's MMORPG [Night Crows](/game/nightcrows-5th-class-20260716) receives a major second-half update on July 16. Developer MADNGINE says the content was shaped by player feedback gathered after the third-anniversary update. The centerpiece is the **5th class advancement**, a new progression stage for top-end characters who have finished their 4th awakening, which extends the enhancement tiers for heroic and legendary skills and adds a shared healing skill usable by every class.

The Guild Hideout arriving alongside it is a guild-only space offering buff NPCs and cooperative boss summoning content. On the progression side, a higher-grade Immortal Element made by combining five mythic elements is being introduced, and the existing merit insignia ranking is being reworked into a new ranking that reflects actual PvP activity. MADNGINE noted that this reveal covers only part of the update, and that the frequently requested class reboot and rebalancing will follow in stages.
