The Korean edition of Nihon Falcom's action RPG [Kyoto Xanadu](/game/kyoto-xanadu-blooming-phantom-20260716) releases on July 16. Supported platforms are PlayStation 5, Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and Steam, with Clouded Leopard Entertainment handling domestic distribution. The standard edition is priced at 79,300 won for both download and physical copies, while the limited edition packed with bonus items runs 103,700 won.

The game presents itself as a coming-of-age fantasy set in the old capital of Kyoto, following the ordinary and extraordinary lives of awakened teenagers. Its defining structural feature is a dual dimensional ARPG concept that moves between classic side-view traversal and 3D battles. The limited edition includes a special art box depicting old Kyoto along with bonus items.
