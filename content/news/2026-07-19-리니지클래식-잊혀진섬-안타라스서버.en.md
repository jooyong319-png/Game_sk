NCSoft is rolling out a new episode, "Forgotten Island," alongside a fresh server named "Antaras" in its PC MMORPG [Lineage Classic](/game/lineage-classic-server-antaras-forgotten-island-20260722) on July 22. Set in an uncharted region southeast of the Aden continent, Forgotten Island introduces new monsters including minotaurs, drakes, griffons and crocodiles. The Antaras server starts everyone from the same line, letting new and returning players jump in without falling behind.

Pre-registration runs from July 15 through August 19. Players on existing servers receive a "Forgotten Island Treasure Chest," while Antaras players get a "Welcome Treasure Chest" as their reward. The first round of server transfers will also be accepted in two waves, from July 22 to August 4.
