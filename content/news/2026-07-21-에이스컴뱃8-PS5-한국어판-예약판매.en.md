Bandai Namco Entertainment Korea announced that pre-orders for the PS5 Korean-language package edition of [Ace Combat 8: Wings of Sieve](/game/ace-combat-8-wings-of-theve-2026) open on Tuesday, July 21. Everyone who pre-orders receives a bonus original challenge coin engraved with the protagonist's emblem. The title is the newest entry in the Ace Combat series, whose cumulative sales have topped 21 million copies, and casts players as ace pilots soaring through immersive aerial combat.

The developers said they used their proprietary middleware engine "Cloudly" to render multilayered cloud structures along with atmospheric light effects such as Brocken spectres and god rays. Further details and pre-order guidance are available on Bandai Namco Entertainment Korea's official website.
