Smilegate announced it will hold an online live showcase on July 23 detailing its new MMORPG [Eclipse: The Awakening](/game/eclipse-awakening-showcase-20260723). Developed by Npixel with Smilegate handling global publishing, the game is expected to reveal its previously undisclosed content and design direction for the first time at this showcase.

The official site had already introduced the game's setting, key regions, and the Sanctum system that anchors character progression. Sanctum accumulates various growth materials over time, which players spend on summons and equipment enhancement, and it is structured so that players can shape their progression path around their own play preferences.
