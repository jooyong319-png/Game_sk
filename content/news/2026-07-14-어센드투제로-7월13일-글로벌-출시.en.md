Ascend to ZERO, a time-manipulation action roguelike developed by Krafton subsidiary Flyway Games, launched globally on July 13 for PC via Steam and Xbox Series X/S. It joined the Xbox Game Pass day one lineup at release, meaning subscribers can play it immediately at no extra cost.

The game is set on an Earth destroyed by an alien attack, where the last survivor travels back in time to undo the collapse. A 30-second time limit and the ability to freeze that clock form the core loop, producing tactical combat where players reposition and set up counterattacks while time is stopped. RPG layers such as gear farming and enhancement plus progression across six avatars round out the roguelike repetition. It also stands out as a console and PC release from Krafton outside the battle royale genre.
