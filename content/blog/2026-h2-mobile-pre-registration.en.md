In mobile games, **pre-registration** is more than signing up for a notification. Launch-day rewards scale with how many people register, and for high-profile titles the count itself is an early read on how the launch will go. Here are the Korean mobile releases lined up for the second half of 2026. Each entry links to its schedule, developer and platform details.

## Closest to launch

- [Requiem M](/game/requiem-m-2026) (Q2 2026 target · Gravity) — A dark fantasy MMORPG reinterpreting the original IP for mobile. An early-second-half release looks most likely.
- [Pokemon Champions](/game/pokemon-champions-mobile-2026) (The Pokemon Company) — A new entry focused purely on Pokemon battles. As a global IP, interest was high well before launch.

## Big IPs going mobile

- [ODIN Q](/game/odin-q-2026) (Q3 2026 target · Lionheart Studio) — A new project from the studio behind Odin: Valhalla Rising, supporting PC and mobile cross-platform play.
- [Lost Ark Mobile](/game/lost-ark-mobile-2026) (Smilegate RPG) — The hack-and-slash combat proven on PC, moved to mobile. A deep existing fanbase means heavy pre-registration demand.
- [Night Crows 2](/game/nightcrow2-2026) (Madngine) — The sequel to global hit Night Crows, drawing attention as the follow-up to a well-executed original.

## Subculture and new IPs

- [Azur Promilia](/game/azur-promilia-2026) (Manjuu) — An open-world title from the Azur Lane developer, combining collection and exploration systems. A notable subculture prospect.
- [Eclipse: The Awakening](/game/eclipse-awakening-2026) (NPIXEL) — A new IP from the Gran Saga studio, targeting a multiplatform release.

## Other second-half mobile prospects

- [Solo Leveling: KARMA](/game/solo-leveling-karma-2026) (Netmarble) — Built on the globally successful Solo Leveling IP, with pre-registration interest riding on the source material's popularity.
- [Higan: Eruthyll](/game/higan-eruthyll-kr-2026) — A collectible subculture RPG aiming at enthusiasts with distinctive characters and artwork.
- [DAVE THE DIVER (mobile)](/game/dave-the-diver-mobile-2026) (Nexon) — The mobile version of the global hit, carrying proven design into a portable format.

## Getting the most out of pre-registration

- **Check the rewards**: Milestone rewards differ by game, so read the official channels alongside the listing.
- **Set a launch reminder**: Adding a game to your favorites means you won't miss the date.
- **Confirm the platform**: Many recent titles support PC-mobile cross-play, so it's worth checking that it fits your setup in advance.
