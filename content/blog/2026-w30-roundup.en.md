July is peak season for new mobile games, with idle RPGs, hardcore MMORPGs and sports titles all landing in the same window. Below is a genre-and-date breakdown of the confirmed Korean releases this month, followed by the major IP games arriving later in the year. If you are deciding what to pre-register for, working down this list is a good way to start.

## Confirmed hits launching this month

The one to watch first is [Cookie Run: Crumble](/game/cookierun-crumble-20260729) from Studio Kingdom. This idle RPG lets your cookies grow even when you are not playing, while adding real strategy through cookie-and-pet team building. Global pre-registration opened on June 29 and the full launch lands July 29, making it the month's closing headliner.

For a classic MMORPG, Gravity's [Ragnarok M: Classic](/game/ragnarok-m-classic-20260716) arrived on PC and mobile on July 16. It rebuilds the Zeny-driven economy and the six foundational classes to put the focus back on pure farming. Gravity's dark-fantasy MMORPG [Requiem M](/game/requiem-m-2026), out June 30, leans hard into gory visuals and hardcore combat for players who want an edgier tone.

If you prefer collection and raising, Bandai Namco's [Digimon UP](/game/digimon-up-20260715) went global on July 15, pairing freshly drawn 2D pixel-art Digimon with turn-based battles and evolution. Strategy fans have [Sangokushi: Tenka Kessen](/game/samgukji-chunhagyeoljeon-20260702) from Bilibili Games, launched July 2 with over a million pre-registrations and a monetization model that drops resource, speed-up and VIP sales.

## Recent June launches still worth starting now

Netmarble's [Sol: Enchant](/game/sol-enchant-20260618) is a god-themed MMORPG that went live June 18 with PC and mobile cross-play. If you want competitive battles, the mobile version of [Pokemon Champions](/game/pokemon-champions-mobile-2026) launched June 17 with save data and cross-play tied to the Switch edition, focused on free-to-play ranked battles. IP fans should note Netmarble's open-world action RPG [Game of Thrones: Kingsroad](/game/got-kingsroad-20260514), which skips gacha in favor of a pass-based model.

## Coming in August and the second half

August 6 brings NetEase's [NBA Dunk City](/game/nba-dunk-city-20260806), an officially licensed street-basketball game with 40-plus real NBA players and a MOBA-style approach to raising and combining players. Further out, the year's headline IP titles are Netmarble Neo's roguelite action game [Solo Leveling: Karma](/game/solo-leveling-karma-2026) and the Unreal Engine 5 cross-play remake [Lost Ark Mobile](/game/lost-ark-mobile-2026).
