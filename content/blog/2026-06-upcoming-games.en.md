June 2026 is **a month where Korean mobile launches, global blockbusters and MMORPG server openings all arrive together.** Below are the major dates from mid-June through the end of the month, grouped by category. Every title links to details and its remaining D-day count. For the wider view, see [Upcoming Games](/upcoming-games) and [New Servers & Events](/new-servers).

## New Korean mobile releases

The month's biggest mobile launch is [SOL: enchant](/game/sol-enchant-20260618). Netmarble's MMORPG built around a deity concept releases June 18, with combat and progression systems drawing on divine powers. [Pokemon Champions](/game/pokemon-champions-mobile-2026) joins on June 17 as a battle-focused entry in a global IP, and its competitive design had people watching well before launch. At the end of the month, Gravity's dark fantasy MMORPG [Requiem M](/game/requiem-m-2026) is in its final stretch toward a June 30 target.

## Global blockbusters

The global lineup is dense too. [Final Fantasy Tactics – The Ivalice Chronicles](/game/ff-tactics-ivalice-chronicles-2026) brings a Square Enix SRPG landmark to current hardware on June 17 across multiple platforms, with the original's political drama and tactical combat carried over intact. The following day, June 18, [Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales](/game/adventures-of-elliot-millennium-tales-2026) arrives as a charming exploration adventure. Sports and fighting fans should note [EA Sports UFC 6](/game/ea-sports-ufc-6-2026) on June 19 and [Dead or Alive 6: Last Round](/game/doa6-last-round-20260625) on June 25. UFC 6 refines its striking and grappling animation; Last Round bundles all DLC and ships alongside a free base version.

## Korean MMORPG servers and events

Late June is especially tight with new servers and major updates. It opens June 17 with the [Prasia Chronicles 2026 Summer Festival](/game/prasia-summer-festival-20260617), followed June 18 by [MapleStory's new class Rethe](/game/maplestory-class-rethe-20260618) in the first summer OVERDRIVE update. On June 20, the [Lost Ark 2026 LOA ON Summer](/game/lostark-loaon-summer-20260620) showcase reveals the second-half roadmap. June 24 puts two openings head to head: [Odin: Valhalla Rising's 5th anniversary Skadi server](/game/odin-server-skadi-20260624) and the [Lineage M PHOENIX update with its new Reboot World](/game/lineage-m-server-phoenix-20260624). Add [Mabinogi Mobile Season 2 "Light and Dark"](/game/mabinogi-mobile-season2-light-dark-20260625) on June 25, and the best entry points for new and returning players are all concentrated in this month.
