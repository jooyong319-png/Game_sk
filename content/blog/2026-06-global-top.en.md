Summer 2026 is **a season where series blockbusters, remasters and expansions all land at once.** From late June through the end of August, here's the global PC and console slate, sorted by month. Every title links to full details and its remaining D-day count. To look further ahead, browse [Upcoming Games](/upcoming-games) and [Global Blockbusters](/global-games).

## June — opening the summer

A Square Enix SRPG landmark returns to current hardware. [Final Fantasy Tactics – The Ivalice Chronicles](/game/ff-tactics-ivalice-chronicles-2026) launches June 17 across PC, PS5 and Switch, with the original's political drama and tactical combat intact — which is the whole point of the release.

Arriving the same week, [Adventures of Elliot: The Millennium Tales](/game/adventures-of-elliot-millennium-tales-2026) joins on June 18 as a new Square Enix adventure built around charming visuals and exploration-first design. Sports and fighting game fans have [EA Sports UFC 6](/game/ea-sports-ufc-6-2026) on June 19 and [Dead or Alive 6: Last Round](/game/doa6-last-round-20260625) on June 25. UFC 6 leads with refined striking and grappling animation; Last Round's pitch is the complete package with all DLC bundled in.

## July — the big IPs come back in sequence

July is stacked with major series entries. Nintendo's [Star Fox (Switch 2)](/game/star-fox-switch2-2026) revives the series on July 2 with the new hardware's performance behind it, and on July 9 [Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced](/game/ac-black-flag-resynced-2026) returns with a modern rebuild of the pirate-action high point.

The same day brings [Granblue Fantasy: Relink – Endless Ragnarok](/game/granblue-relink-endless-ragnarok-2026), an expansion adding large-scale endgame content to a well-received action RPG. [Sword Art Online: Echoes of Aincrad](/game/sao-echoes-of-aincrad-20260710) joins on July 10 aimed squarely at fans of the source, and late July holds [Splatoon Raiders](/game/splatoon-raiders-2026) (July 23) and one of the half's biggest releases, [Halo: Campaign Evolved](/game/halo-campaign-evolved-20260728) (July 28). Halo reinterprets the series' origin for modern hardware, targeting newcomers and veterans at the same time.

## August — new IPs alongside classic collections

August pairs fresh swings with nostalgia. [Beast of Reincarnation](/game/beast-of-reincarnation-2026), from Pokemon studio Game Freak, arrives August 4 as a surprising turn into dark fantasy action, and on August 6 [Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls](/game/marvel-tokon-2026) renders a Marvel hero brawl through Arc System Works' signature presentation.

Realism-FPS benchmark [Hell Let Loose: Vietnam](/game/hell-let-loose-vietnam-2026) adds a Vietnam War setting to its 50-vs-50 battles on August 13. August 27 brings a double launch: [Metal Gear Solid Master Collection Vol. 2](/game/mgs-master-collection-vol2-2026) and Respawn's new strategy title [Star Wars: Zero Company](/game/star-wars-zero-company-20260827). The next day, August 28, [Elden Ring Tarnished Edition (Switch 2)](/game/elden-ring-tarnished-edition-switch2-20260828) closes the summer by putting a soulslike landmark in your hands.

## The summer at a glance

From the late-June Square Enix run through July's Halo and Assassin's Creed to August's mix of new IP and classic collections, summer 2026 covers nearly every genre. Heavyweights like [The Blood of Dawnwalker](/game/blood-of-dawnwalker-20260903) are queued for September onward, so mark anything you're tracking on the [release calendar](/upcoming-games).
