# Late June 2026: What to Lock In Before It Ships

This week stacks imminent releases against titles taking their final pre-registrations. The spread runs from major Korean mobile MMORPG updates to console headliners, across genres and platforms alike. Games with closing pre-registration windows usually have rewards attached, so signing up before launch is simply the better deal. Everything below is ordered by how close the D-day is.

## Shipping within 14 days

Start with the two MMORPG updates colliding on **June 24.** [Odin: Valhalla Rising's 5th anniversary Skadi server](/game/odin-server-skadi-20260624) marks five years of service by adding the new Sorceress advancement Alchemist and world stronghold sieges at the same time. Pre-registration closes **June 23** — one day left, so move quickly. The same day, the [Lineage M PHOENIX update and new Reboot World](/game/lineage-m-server-phoenix-20260624) delivers its ninth-anniversary patch with an Elf class reboot and a new world. If you're aiming at the early game on a fresh server, the fact that both start from the same line is worth noting.

On console, **June 25** brings [Dead or Alive 6: Last Round](/game/doa6-last-round-20260625) back as an expanded edition with a 29-fighter roster including five DLC characters. A free base version launches the same day, which keeps the barrier to entry low. Also on the 25th, [Mabinogi Mobile Season 2 "Light and Dark"](/game/mabinogi-mobile-season2-light-dark-20260625) applies a major seasonal update with new story and dungeons.

Month's end and early July stay packed. [Requiem M](/game/requiem-m-2026), Gravity's dark fantasy mobile MMORPG, is in its final stretch toward a Q2 target. On **July 1**, [Aion 2 Chapter 1 "Land of Sand and Frost"](/game/aion2-chapter1-sand-frost-20260701) opens the game's first major chapter with a level 50 cap expansion, the new Gunsage class and new Elyos and Asmodian regions. The same day, [Genshin Impact 6.7 "Luna VIII"](/game/genshin-6-7-luna-viii-20260701) rolls out globally with new five-star Sandrone, modeled on the seventh of the Fatui Harbingers.

Console releases follow in sequence. On **July 2**, [Star Fox (Switch 2)](/game/star-fox-switch2-2026) arrives as a cinematic remake of 1997's Star Fox 64, a Switch 2 exclusive with Joy-Con 2 mouse controls and an orchestral soundtrack. The same day, [Avatar Legends: The Fighting Game](/game/avatar-legends-fighting-game-2026) launches across all platforms — a 1v1 fighter built on the Last Airbender and Legend of Korra IPs with rollback netcode and full cross-play.

## Mid-July, opening for preorder soon

Looking slightly further out, the second week of July is solid. On **July 9-10**, [Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced](/game/ac-black-flag-resynced-2026) returns as a full remake in the current Anvil engine with a new ending and added content, while [Granblue Fantasy: Relink – Endless Ragnarok](/game/granblue-relink-endless-ragnarok-2026) arrives as a large expansion with new story, summons and cross-play. In the same window, [Sword Art Online: Echoes of Aincrad](/game/sao-echoes-of-aincrad-20260710) lets players dive into Aincrad as their own avatar for the first time in the series — a big draw for fans of the source. If you'd rather play handheld, the [Digimon Story: Time Stranger (Switch)](/game/digimon-time-stranger-switch-2026) port joins the same day. Baseball fans should hold July 16 for [eBaseball: PRO SPIRIT 2026](/game/pro-spirit-2026), which brings the series' first Korean subtitles and a WBC mode.

## Wrapping up

This week comes down to two things: the closing pre-registration windows for the Odin and Lineage M servers, and the run of console headliners scheduled through early July. More upcoming titles are on [Upcoming Games](/upcoming-games) and [New Servers & Events](/new-servers), with platform breakdowns and D-day counts on [Mobile Games](/mobile-games), [PC & Console Games](/pc-console-games) and [Global Blockbusters](/global-games). Release and pre-registration dates can move, so register early for anything you care about and let the calendar notify you.
