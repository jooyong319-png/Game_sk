New server openings and major updates are **the moments live games pull in the most new and returning players.** Rewards are larger and the competitive field resets, so missing the start makes catching up genuinely hard. Here are the major server openings and events scheduled from late June through August 2026, in date order. Each entry links to its exact schedule and D-day count.

## Late June — a crowded week for new servers

- [Odin: Valhalla Rising 5th anniversary server "Skadi"](/game/odin-server-skadi-20260624) (June 24) — A new world for the fifth anniversary. Early land-grab competition is fierce, so joining on day one matters.
- [Lineage M "PHOENIX" update and new Reboot World](/game/lineage-m-server-phoenix-20260624) (June 24) — Reboot Worlds run under different rules from standard servers, which effectively gives new and returning players a level start.
- [Mabinogi Mobile Season 2 "Light and Dark"](/game/mabinogi-mobile-season2-light-dark-20260625) (June 25) — Seasonal content resets here, making this the easiest point to enter.
- [EOS Black 2nd anniversary boost servers "Maroon" and "Ruth Camp"](/game/eos-black-server-maroon-ruth-camp-20260625) (June 25) — Boost servers accelerate progression, so catching up to the main game takes far less time.

## July — big live-game updates

- [Aion 2 Chapter 1 "Land of Sand and Frost"](/game/aion2-chapter1-sand-frost-20260701) (July 1) — The game's first major content drop since launch, opening the main story and a new region.
- [Genshin Impact 6.7 "Luna VIII"](/game/genshin-6-7-luna-viii-20260701) (July 1) — A regular version update with a new character and region, and a good moment to collect returning-player rewards alongside it.
- [Lost Ark's new Dimensioner class](/game/lostark-class-dimensioner-20260708) (July 8) — New class releases are among Lost Ark's biggest inflow events, and they usually arrive with leveling support events attached.
- [Honkai: Star Rail x Fate second collaboration](/game/hsr-fate-collab-holy-grail-20260724) (July 24) — Big IP collaborations live and die on limited characters and events, so participating inside the window is the whole point.

## Early August — endgame content

- [Lost Ark 8-player Shadow Raid "Belgardin"](/game/lostark-raid-belgardin-20260805) (August 5) — A new top-tier raid. Prepare gear and a roster in advance and a first-week clear is realistic.

## The takeaway — day one is what counts

New servers and major updates concentrate **their rewards and positional advantage on opening day,** so it pays to check dates ahead of time for anything you care about.
