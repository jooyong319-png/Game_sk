Late July is quietly stacked with **no-gacha single-player action** rather than blockbuster console tentpoles. Between July 23 and 31, five Korean and indie action RPGs land within ten days, and at a glance they all look like the same "swing a sword" game. They actually play very differently, so here they are sorted by taste. If you want a wider view of console and PC releases, the [PC & Console Games](/pc-console-games) page is worth a look too.

## For sprawling open-world play - Dragon Sword Awakening

The headliner of this lineup is [Dragon Sword Awakening](/game/dragonsword-awakening-20260723). Developer Hound13 ended its old publishing deal and pivoted to a **no-gacha, buy-to-play package**, an anime-style open-world action RPG launching globally on Steam on July 23. You recruit distinct companions for flashy tag-team combat while exploring a full continent, making it the biggest and most freeform of the five. It suits players who want to roam for days rather than grind through a tight, short soulslike.

## When combos and boss fights come first - Dinoblade vs The Relic

If you want proper soulslike feel, two titles collide head-on. [Dinoblade](/game/dinoblade-20260723) also arrives July 23, with the offbeat premise of playing a young Spinosaurus swinging a greatsword. Its dodge-roll-parry combo combat plus Korean subtitles keep the barrier low. [The Relic: First Guardian](/game/the-relic-first-guardian-20260731), meanwhile, launches July 31 on PS5 and PC first, a **dark fantasy reimagining Korean folklore** where you build your character through gear and relics instead of levels. Think light and inventive for Dinoblade, heavy and build-focused for The Relic.

## When you want gunplay and multiplayer - Kusan and Mistfall Hunter

If you would rather shoot as well as slash, [Kusan: City of Wolves](/game/kusan-city-of-wolves-20260730) is the pick. Built over eight years by Circle From Dot, this hardcore top-down shooter fuses Hotline Miami pace with John Wick-style melee-and-gun chaining, with a soundtrack by hip-hop producer Loptimist. It launches July 30 on PC, PS5, Xbox, and Switch, the widest platform reach here. Prefer company over solo? [Mistfall Hunter](/game/mistfall-hunter-20260729) layers a PvPvE extraction structure and crossplay onto soulslike combat, releasing worldwide on July 29 so you can meet friends on the same server.

## Lighter alternatives with less commitment

If a big time sink feels like too much, there are quicker options. Krafton studio Flyway Games' [Ascend to Zero](/game/ascend-to-zero-20260713) is a time-stopping roguelike already out since July 13, budget-priced and available day one on Game Pass. And if you want something genuinely different to control, the rhythm action of [Ratatan](/game/ratatan-2026) from the Patapon team is a great change of pace.

In short: Dragon Sword for open exploration, Dinoblade or The Relic for soulslike feel, Kusan or Mistfall Hunter for gunplay and multiplayer, Ascend to Zero or Ratatan for something light.
