Even with console and mobile releases arriving in volume, the **PC MMORPG** remains a defining genre in the Korean market. 2026 has several established studios lining up new PC MMORPGs, which makes it a welcome year for anyone who has been waiting on a proper PC MMO. Here's what's coming.

## Direct sequels to major IPs

- [Aion 2 Global](/game/aion2-global-2026) (September 2026 target · NCSoft) — The official sequel to Aion. PC-first with a simultaneous global launch as the goal, and the thing to watch is how it carries over the original's identity, aerial combat in particular.
- [ArcheAge Chronicles](/game/archeage-chronicles-2026) (November 2026 target · XLGames) — The next entry in the ArcheAge line, supporting PC and console (PS5, Xbox). Its high-freedom sandbox systems are what's generating expectations.

## New IPs and next-gen swings

- [Chrono Odyssey](/game/chrono-odyssey-2026) (November 2026 target · NPIXEL) — A new IP drawing attention for its Unreal Engine 5 visuals. Combat and exploration are built around time manipulation, with a simultaneous PC and console release in preparation.
- [MIR5](/game/mir5-2026) (targeting within 2026 · Wemade Next) — A numbered entry in the MIR series. The schedule remains fluid, but development is aimed at a release inside the year.

## Other Korean PC and console releases worth tracking

Beyond traditional MMORPGs, the second half of 2026 has Korean PC and console releases spread widely.

- [RF Online Next – new region "Lavaron"](/game/rf-online-next-lavaron-20260701) (July 1 · Webzen) — The successor to the long-beloved sci-fi MMORPG expands with a large new region update.
- [Dragonsword: Awakening](/game/dragonsword-awakening-20260723) (July 23) — An action RPG themed on awakening, with flashy PC-based combat presentation as its hook.
- [Kusan: City of Wolves](/game/kusan-city-of-wolves-20260730) (July 30 · Circle From Dot) — A hardcore top-down shooter eight years in development, set in a world that reimagines Korean traditional culture through a futurist lens.
- [The Relic: First Guardian](/game/the-relic-first-guardian-20260731) (July 31) — A Korean PC release billed as an action adventure built around relics.

## What to look for in 2026's PC MMORPGs

This year's PC MMORPG field splits into **sequels to proven IPs and new IPs on next-gen engines.** Aion 2 and ArcheAge Chronicles target existing fanbases; Chrono Odyssey aims at new players with fresh visuals and systems.
